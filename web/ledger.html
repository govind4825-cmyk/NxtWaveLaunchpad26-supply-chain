How these numbers are made. Need = people × Sphere minimum standards × days until the alert ends plus two days, minus what is already ordered or delivered. People come from each shelter's latest headcount; where none has arrived yet, an estimate from WorldPop 2020 population in the area the shelter serves × a displacement planning rate set by the district officer (marked "estimate"). Shelters whose last safe road closes soonest are listed first.

Sources: official alerts (NDMA SACHET, IMD, GDACS), WorldPop, OpenStreetMap, shelter headcounts. Machine-readable: /api/ledger. Refreshes every minute.
